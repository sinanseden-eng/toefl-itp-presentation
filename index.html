TOEFL ITP® LEVEL 1 GUIDE

Prep is course — a seven-card tour through the test format, scoring, and section-by-section strategy. Flip through the deck at your own pace.

or press → / Enter

ESSENTIALS

A paper-based, all-multiple-choice test of academic English — used for placement, scholarships and exchange screening.

  • Three sections, 140 questions, about 115 minutes.
  • Answers go on a separate answer sheet — practice bubbling.
  • No speaking or writing tasks; scores valid for two years.

FORMAT

Listening Comprehension50 Q35 min
Structure & Written Expression40 Q25 min
Reading Comprehension50 Q55 min
ITP vs iBT: ITP is paper-based and set by your institution; the iBT adds speaking & writing online.

SCORING

Each section scores 20–68; the total (310–677) is the sum × 10 ÷ 3.

627–677 · C1543–626 · B2460–542 · B1337–459 · A2
No penalty for wrong answers — an educated guess always beats a blank.

LISTENING

Audio plays once at natural speed; questions aren't printed in the book.

  • Part A: the second speaker usually holds the answer; beware sound-alikes.
  • Parts B & C: preview choices while the Directions read.
  • Miss one? Guess instantly and refocus — the audio won't wait.

STRUCTURE

40 grammar questions in two formats:

  • Structure (15): fill the blank — check subject–verb balance first.
  • Written Expression (25): spot the one underlined error.
Usual suspects: agreement, parallelism, word form, missing or extra connectors.

READING

50 questions across 5–6 academic passages; answer in any order.

  • Skim topic sentences before diving into detail.
  • Vocabulary items: plug the option back into the sentence.
  • Reference items: find the nearest logical antecedent.
  • Pace at ~11 minutes per passage; flag and return.

STRATEGY

  • Rehearse with timed, paper-format practice tests.
  • Wear a watch — phones stay away; budget per section.
  • Answer every question; blanks score zero, guesses can't.
  • Sleep well, arrive early, bring ID and two pencils.
You're set — know the format, trust your pacing, and let consistent practice finish the job.
